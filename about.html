About XferLogic

Trading intelligence.
Human + machine.

XferLogic is being built around a simple idea: trading technology gets better when software intelligence and real practitioner experience reinforce each other.

Product

ChelsieAI

AI-assisted trading operations designed around visibility, explainability, configurable controls, measurable performance, and safer releases.

Professional Network

XferLogic Community

A domain-specific professional graph for ETRM specialists: structured Experience Passports, technical Q&A, jobs/projects, and moderated knowledge.

What XferLogic is trying to solve

Complex systems fail when knowledge, controls, and ownership become disconnected.

Make software understandable

Trading automation should expose operating state, constraints, decisions, and evidence instead of behaving like a black box.

Make expertise discoverable

ETRM profiles should reveal what someone has actually implemented, supported, troubleshot, and owned—not merely where they worked.

Make knowledge reusable

Lessons learned should become curated resources rather than disappearing into inboxes, project folders, and individual memory.

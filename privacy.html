Privacy Policy

How XferLogic handles information associated with the website and the private ChelsieAI Execution Evidence — Paper Read Only application.

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy is a general operational document for the current private, paper-only application. It should receive professional legal review before XferLogic offers the application commercially or connects customer live brokerage accounts.

1. Scope

This Policy describes information that XferLogic may process through the XferLogic website and the ChelsieAI Execution Evidence — Paper Read Only application. The current Alpaca integration is limited to paper accounts and read-only reconciliation of orders, fills, and fees. No live-account access is currently part of this application.

2. Information We May Process

Website and contact information. If you choose to contact XferLogic or submit information through an enabled website form, we may process the information you provide, such as your name, email address, organization, areas of interest, and message content.

OAuth authorization metadata and access credentials. The application may process connection and authorization metadata and, when issued by the connection flow, access credentials or tokens necessary to maintain the limited paper-account connection. Such credentials are intended to be stored using reasonable technical safeguards and used only for the authorized connection. The current application requests no OAuth scopes; trading, account:write, and data are prohibited. The application does not request market-data access.

Paper-account execution evidence. For reconciliation, the application may process paper-account order details, fill details, fees, timestamps, statuses, and related activity information made available through Alpaca.

Sanitized technical, security, and provenance records. XferLogic may process sanitized logs or records needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, validate, and document the evidence-reconciliation process. These may include timestamps, software versions, reconciliation status, error information, control results, source provenance, and similar technical records.

Website analytics and cookies. The XferLogic website currently uses Google Analytics to understand website usage and traffic. Analytics technologies may process information such as browser or device information, approximate location derived from network information, page views, referral information, and cookie or similar identifiers, subject to the provider’s configuration and policies.

3. Why We Process Information

Information is processed as reasonably necessary to provide and secure the Service, authenticate and maintain authorized connections, reconcile paper-account execution evidence, investigate errors, maintain provenance and operational records, improve usability and reliability, understand website traffic, respond to inquiries, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Credential and Token Handling

Do not submit Alpaca passwords, access tokens, API credentials, or other brokerage secrets through ordinary XferLogic website forms, email messages, community posts, or support messages. XferLogic does not ask users to provide their Alpaca password. Authorization credentials should be exchanged only through the designated connection mechanism and handled according to the application’s security controls.

5. Data Minimization and Evidence Adapter Behavior

The evidence adapter is designed to minimize persisted brokerage identifiers. Raw Alpaca account, order, and activity identifiers are not persisted by the evidence adapter. Such identifiers may be processed transiently when necessary to retrieve, correlate, or reconcile source records, but the adapter does not retain those raw identifiers as part of its persisted evidence output.

6. Data Security

XferLogic applies reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the current private application, including access controls, limited-purpose credentials, restricted application scope, and sanitized evidence records. No security measure can eliminate all risk, and XferLogic does not guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

XferLogic seeks to retain personal information, connection metadata, sanitized technical records, and reconciliation evidence only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this Policy, operational continuity, security, troubleshooting, record integrity, or applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by data category and should be refined through professional legal and operational review before commercial launch.

8. Restricted Disclosure and Service Providers

XferLogic does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be disclosed on a limited basis to service providers that support hosting, authentication, analytics, security, communications, or related technical operations, where reasonably necessary for those services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or security, or with the user’s direction or consent.

9. Alpaca as an Independent Third Party

Alpaca is an independent third-party provider and is not owned or controlled by XferLogic. Alpaca processes information under its own terms and privacy practices. XferLogic is not responsible for Alpaca’s independent privacy, security, retention, availability, or account practices.

10. User Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the nature of your relationship with XferLogic, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you by contacting XferLogic. Some records may need to be retained where reasonably required for security, integrity, legal obligations, or dispute prevention.

You may stop using the Service and revoke or disconnect applicable authorization through the relevant Alpaca or application connection controls. Revocation prevents future authorized access but may not automatically delete records that were lawfully retained before revocation.

11. Children’s Privacy

The website and application are intended for adults and professional users and are not directed to children. XferLogic does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this Service. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact XferLogic so the matter can be reviewed.

12. Policy Updates

This Policy may be updated as the Service, legal requirements, security controls, or data practices evolve. Updated versions will identify a revised effective date.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to hello@xferlogic.com.

Professional legal review is recommended before commercial use, customer live-account connectivity, or any expansion beyond the current private paper-only, read-only scope.

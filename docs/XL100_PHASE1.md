# XL100 Phase 1

## Positioning
XL100 is the enterprise ETRM customer network within XferLogic.

Eligibility is organization-based and vendor-neutral. A company qualifies when it self-declares at least 100 installed/user ETRM licenses across one or more commercial ETRM/CTRM products. License counts are honor-system declarations and are reviewed/approved by an XL100 administrator.

## Organization tiers
- XL100 Member: 100–299 licenses
- XL100 Gold: 300–499 licenses
- XL100 Elite: 500+ licenses

Tier is assigned from the administrator-approved license band. The exact license count does not need to be public.

## Phase 1 capabilities
1. Organization application
2. Admin approval / reject / request-info workflow
3. Tier assignment and override
4. Member registration and sign-in
5. Organization profile
6. Experience Passport
7. Role-based access (member / company admin / moderator / XL100 admin)

## Organization application fields
- Legal / display name
- Website
- Headquarters country
- Operating regions
- Industry / commodities
- ETRM products/vendors used
- Self-declared license band
- Applicant name, role, corporate email
- Short description / why they want to join
- Terms acknowledgment

## Application status
- draft
- submitted
- under_review
- needs_info
- approved
- rejected

## Experience Passport
- Title / role
- Company
- Location
- Years of ETRM experience
- Platforms used hands-on
- Commodities
- Lifecycle / functional domains
- Implementation roles
- Go-live experience
- Production support experience
- Data migration experience
- Integration experience
- Testing / cutover experience
- Critical narrative questions

### Critical questions
1. Which ETRM processes can you explain end-to-end without relying on another SME?
2. Describe the hardest production issue you personally diagnosed and how you proved the root cause.
3. What did you personally own during your most recent go-live?
4. Which integrations have you designed or troubleshot hands-on?

## Governance
- One company = one roadmap vote per roadmap item (future phase).
- Tier affects prestige / representative capacity, not voting weight.
- Vendor participation will be a separate account type in a later phase.
- No confidential commercial terms, trading positions, contract pricing, or procurement coordination.

## Public labels
- XL100 Approved
- XL100 Gold
- XL100 Elite

The public profile does not need to expose exact license count.

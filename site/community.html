Moderated Knowledge Library

Share what works.
Keep the library trustworthy.

Reusable resources go through a visible moderation workflow before becoming an approved community asset.

01 · Submit

Member contributes a checklist, runbook, template, lesson learned, or troubleshooting guide.

02 · Screen

Basic file-safety, secrets, duplicate, and required-metadata checks.

03 · Review

Moderator evaluates relevance, quality, ownership, confidentiality, and usefulness.

04 · Publish

Approved content receives version, tags, contributor attribution, and review status.

05 · Improve

Feedback and revisions return through review before replacing the approved version.
